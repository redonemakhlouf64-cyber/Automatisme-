Privacy Policy — NYX Social

Last updated: October 17, 2026

About this project

NYX Social is a project under development for managing social media accounts and preparing and publishing content.

Information and account access

Depending on enabled features, the application may process account identifiers, profile and board information, content supplied by users, and authorization tokens needed to connect to social platforms.

Use of information

Information is intended to be used only to provide the account management and publishing features requested by the user. Platform access is subject to the relevant platform's permissions and policies.

Storage and security

Storage and security depend on the deployed version and its configuration. Before connecting real accounts, the operator must implement appropriate safeguards for credentials and tokens and restrict access to them.

Sharing

When a user requests publishing, content may be sent to the selected social platform. This project does not claim that integrations are active unless they have been implemented and authorized.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project operator through the repository where this project is published.
